About Us

CPWF APPROACH team has developed and beening promoted the application of the CPWF quantum chemical calculation model and AI algorithms. It provides efficient, accurate, and scalable computational simulation and optimization solutions for areas such as innovative drug design for biomacromolecules and the development of new solid and nanocrystalline materials.
